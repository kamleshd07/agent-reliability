## Finding (diagnostic on 3 synthetic cases, raw replies inspected; synthetic text only)

The replies are well-formed JSON in the `gentlity.judge-output/1` schema. **Groundedness replies were accepted.** The failures were on **relevance**: the model returned
`"citations":[0]` although relevance has no context blocks, so the judge correctly rejected the reply as invalid output (`JudgeInvalidOutputError`). That is a
model-behaviour and prompt-compatibility issue, not truncation or a broken endpoint.

## Against the approved internal thresholds

The approved evaluator-failure ceiling is 2 % (`max_evaluator_failure_rate`). **As configured, Qwen3-14B does not meet it** (22-42 % in the smoke runs). Under the
rules in `PREDECLARED_THRESHOLDS.json`, a model that fails the smoke check is replaced or its configuration corrected **before** the held-out run; nothing is tuned on
the evaluation split and the thresholds are not changed. Options for the owner (none done): (1) a separately reviewed, model-agnostic improvement to how the relevance
prompt/validator handle citations; (2) another different-family candidate; (3) accept Qwen3-14B only after a corrected configuration passes a new smoke run on
data that is not the evaluation split.

## Reproduction

Harness: the SDK judge (`SemanticChecks`, `local_runtime_provider`) over `examples.semantic_shopping_evaluation.golden.cases()`, with `reasoning_effort: "none"` injected
for the no-think runs. The raw per-evaluation rows are not committed (no answer text is kept). No answer text was stored; no AWS, hosted inference or paid service was used.
